About the Organic Waste System

This platform serves as the interactive dashboard for the Smart Organic Waste Processing Live Digital Twin. The system digitally tracks physical waste processes from the point of household disposal all the way to anaerobic digestion.

By ensuring accurate segregation of organic waste at the source, you help produce sustainable biogas and nutrient-rich digestate while participating in a community-driven reward ecosystem.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Only Organic Food Waste: Ensure only pure organic food scraps are deposited into your assigned Smart Bin.
  • Earn Credits: Clean deposits, verified by the docking station's secondary sensors, earn credit points. 1kg of organic waste equals 10 credits.
  • Contamination Penalties: If the flatbed scrutiny layer detects inorganic materials (e.g., metals, plastics) or hidden contaminants, the batch is automatically rejected and diverted. You will not receive credits, and a penalty flag will be recorded on your account.
  • Bin Authentication: Your bin is exclusively paired to your user account. Docking stations use NFC tags and BLE 5.0 handshakes to verify ownership before unlocking the hatch.
